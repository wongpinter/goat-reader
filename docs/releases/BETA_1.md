# Frog Reader BETA 1 — upstream release record

**2026-10-11 · Android 8.0+ · versionCode 4 · GPL-3.0-only**

This archived note records the upstream Frog Reader BETA 1 release by KNITIPKA. It describes that upstream build, not a GOAT Reader release.

## Highlights

- Added the Profile, Reading Queue, Reading Log, and Quotes & Bookmarks.
- Added more reader controls, chapter-aware progress, and publisher-rendering improvements.
- Added welcome setup, backup/license access and additional Light/Beige theme polish.

## Limitations

PDF, KFX, DRM-protected books, full fixed-layout EPUB/KF8, vertical writing, and embedded audio/video are unsupported. EPUB CSS, MathML and ruby support are bounded subsets. Device behavior and visual fidelity vary by Android version and publication.
